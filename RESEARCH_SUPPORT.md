# Research Support & Institutional Funding

## Overview

The **Relativistic Scalar Strain (RSS) Architecture** and **Vacuum Stress Saturation (VSS) Monograph Series** are developed as an open-science, first-principles theoretical physics initiative. 

As an independent research effort operating outside traditional university departmental overhead, this project seeks targeted institutional grants, computational resource allocations, philanthropic scientific sponsorships, and formal open-science support to maintain publication schedules, perform non-linear numerical simulations, and maintain open access for all data, derivations, and execution pipelines.

---

## Targeted Use of Funds & Resources

Direct support and grant awards are deployed strictly toward the following operational milestones:

### 1. High-Performance Computing (HPC) & Simulation Infrastructure
* Dedicated GPU/CPU compute time allocations for running $N$-body cosmological simulations, non-linear halo collapse engines, and high-resolution galaxy-lensing cross-correlation calculations ($C_\ell^{g\kappa}$) for Volume III.
* Cloud CI/CD pipeline infrastructure for continuous regression testing and numerical precision validation.

### 2. Open-Access Publication & Academic Dissemination
* Article Processing Charges (APCs) for peer-reviewed open-access journal submissions in primary astrophysics and cosmology journals.
* Travel and registration funding for presenting VSS/RSS theoretical derivations and numerical results at international astrophysics conferences and physics symposiums.

### 3. Peer-Review Honoraria & Scientific Advisory
* Provision of independent peer-review honoraria for external domain experts to perform verification of symbolic math scripts, numerical solvers, and theoretical derivations across Volumes 1–$N$.
